## Conclusions

1. **Saturation is format-specific, not niche-specific.** 10,000 channels in each space — but the crowding is concentrated in *whiteboard-animated personal-finance explainers.* The user's instinct was right about the wall and wrong about its location.
2. **The wall doesn't pay.** $331/mo at 166K subs and 425 videos.
3. **Business/economics-for-professionals is the high-RPM slot**, not consumer personal finance. Modern MBA is the model: 76 videos, $5,666/mo.
4. **The differentiated slot in the same niche pays ~19× per video** (Primate Economics vs Chris Invests).
5. **The frame-based approach is proven three times over** — and the animal-mascot version of it is already closing.
6. **The software-product frame is unoccupied**, and it is native to the user's chosen Remotion SaaS aesthetic.

## Recommended direction
**Business / economics / industry analysis, delivered through a software-product lens**, faceless, long-form, professional audience.

Companies, economies, industries and institutions treated as **products** — dashboards, metrics, version history, changelogs, churn, uptime, failure post-mortems. The SaaS aesthetic stops being decoration and becomes the analytical vocabulary (§N4).
