### The vocabulary that's working
TechButMakeItReal's tags on a 602K-view video: *consulting leverage model, unit economics, AI economics.* **The analytical register we planned is exactly what's performing.**

Recurring title words in the winners: **quietly**, **nobody is talking about**, **completely fake**, **the end of**. All vindication framing — T07/J4 confirmed in the wild.

### ⚠️ Non-obvious finding: two phrases are poisoned terrain
Searches for **"bankrupt"** and **"rise and fall"** came back dominated by AI short-drama farms — *"He fired me for his lovers, not knowing I'm the real boss, now he's bankrupt!"*, thousands of them, 90+ minute runtimes.

Those phrasings now sit in a semantic neighbourhood full of slop. **Avoid "bankrupt" and "rise and fall" in titles and tags** — not because they don't work, but because they place us adjacent to content farms in embeddings and recommendation. Use *collapsed, folded, went to zero, wound down, delisted, insolvent.*

## ⭐ Episode 001 — recommended

### **Chegg**
> **SEV-1 · Duration: Nov 2022 – present · Impact: ~99% of market cap**

**Why this one first, above everything else:**

| Criterion | Why Chegg clears it |
|---|---|
| Something actually broke | A ~$12B company went to near-zero. Not a "how they make money" explainer. |
| Root cause is crisp | One product launched and the business model stopped working. Cleanest causal chain available. |
| Timeline with real numbers | All in public SEC filings and earnings calls — subscriber counts, revenue, guidance withdrawals, the exact earnings call where management first named ChatGPT. |
| **Legally safe** | Every claim is sourced to filings. No allegations, no contested wrongdoing, nobody to defame. Ideal first episode while the channel has no reputation to defend. (Risk #3, channel bible) |
| Validation framing is honest | The audience already suspects it. We supply the receipts. |
| Dashboards write themselves | Stock chart, subscriber curve, revenue vs guidance, a timeline pinned to a single launch date. **The frame is load-bearing** — passes the §1 lens test. |
| Proven demand shape | Same structure as the 602K-view consulting video: *AI quietly dismantled a business model.* We copy the **idea**, not the execution (T04 ↔ T01). |

**Working title candidates** (validation framing, no poisoned words):
- *"Chegg was worth $12 billion. One product launch ended it."*
- *"I was wrong about what killed Chegg"* — only if a genuine reversal emerges in research. **Never fake this.**
- *"Chegg is already dead. Here's the timeline."*

Fraud allegations about named, active, litigious companies are the highest advertiser-suitability risk in the whole slate, and the worst possible thing to publish from a channel with no standing. Revisit after ~episode 15, once monetization is established and there's a track record of sourced work. See [monetization-safety.md](monetization-safety.md).

## Series structure — the nested commitment premise

From the channel bible: alternative context as the primary cow, with a **commitment series nested inside** for defensibility (T06 type 3, highest moat).

> **The Graveyard** — a numbered, permanent archive of post-mortems, with the count on every thumbnail.

TheBeast721 puts a progress counter on every thumbnail so the premise is visible in the packaging. Ours does the same: **POST-MORTEM #001, #002, #003…** It makes the back catalogue itself the product, which is the one thing a competitor starting today cannot copy.

And "the graveyard" is the audience's own word — from the Modern MBA comment *"herbalife and amway should be in this graveyard."* Per T05, the promise should be **in the viewer's own words.**
