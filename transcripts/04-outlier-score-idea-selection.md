## The outlier score

The answer comes down to a single number. It's called the **outlier score**, and it measures exactly how successful a video has been **for a channel.**

Picture a brand new channel. Its first four videos each average around 100 views — very common for new creators. But then the fifth video crushes it: 10,000 views. In the grand scheme of YouTube, 10,000 views is a drop in the ocean. But for that channel, it's a viral moment. That's a **100× outlier** — 100 times more views than normal *for that channel.*

Some version of this has almost certainly happened to you, just on a smaller scale. You've had a video that quietly did a 2×, a 5×, or maybe a 10× outlier compared to your usual numbers. And you probably have no idea why. **You couldn't explain it, so you couldn't repeat it.**

## What's actually happening underneath an outlier

It's not magic, and it's not the algorithm being generous. As always, it's down to the audience.

**An outlier is typically a video that hits a topic with high demand, yet low supply.** Something a large group of people genuinely want to watch that almost nobody is serving well yet. **That gap between what people want and what's actually out there is your opportunity.**
